Is this for me?

For anyone who wants healthier relationships, starting with the one inside.

  • You often feel responsible for other people's feelings, or let their moods decide yours.

  • Saying no is hard, or you say yes and resent it later.

  • You swing between too much and too little: work, spending, emotion, control.

  • Your sense of worth rises and falls with how others treat you.

  • You simply want tools for living with more peace. No diagnosis needed.

Questions

Before you come

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no cost for the class, the handouts or the audio.

Do I need to read the book first?

No. Everything is taught in class. The book list is there if you want to go deeper.

Will I have to share in front of people?

Only if you want to. Small groups are a place to listen as much as to talk.

Is this therapy?

No. It's a class with peer small groups. It works well alongside counseling but doesn't replace it.

What if I miss a week?

Each class has its handout and audio recording on this site, so you can catch up and come back the next week.
